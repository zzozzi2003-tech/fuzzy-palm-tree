Eleven Store v6.4.4

- Automatic Discord Customer role after purchase completion.
- Bank-transfer orders receive the role when an admin marks the order Delivered.
- Free completed orders receive the role immediately.
- Discord role errors never cancel the order; they are logged in the server console and order log.

Required environment variables:
DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN
DISCORD_GUILD_ID
DISCORD_CUSTOMER_ROLE_ID

Discord bot requirements:
- Bot must be a member of the Discord server.
- Bot needs Manage Roles permission.
- Bot role must be above the Customer role in Discord role hierarchy.
- Customer must be in the Discord server when the role is assigned.
