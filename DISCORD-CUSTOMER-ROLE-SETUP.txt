Eleven Store - Automatic Discord Customer Role

1) Create or use a Discord bot in your Discord application.
2) Invite the bot to your Discord server.
3) Give the bot the Manage Roles permission.
4) In Discord Server Settings > Roles, move the bot's role ABOVE the Customer role.
5) Copy the following values into Render Environment (and .env for local use):

DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN=your_bot_token
DISCORD_GUILD_ID=your_discord_server_id
DISCORD_CUSTOMER_ROLE_ID=your_customer_role_id

Behavior:
- Bank transfer: customer receives the role when an admin marks the order Delivered.
- Free completed order: customer receives the role immediately.
- Re-applying the same Discord role is safe/idempotent.
- The customer must already be a member of the Discord server.
- If Discord role assignment fails, the order is NOT cancelled. The reason is logged in the Render/server console.

How to copy IDs in Discord:
Enable Developer Mode in Discord settings, then right-click the server/role and choose Copy ID.
