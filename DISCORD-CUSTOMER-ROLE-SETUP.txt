DISCORD CUSTOMER ROLE SETUP - Eleven Store v6.4.6

IMPORTANT: Set these in Render > Service > Environment. Do not rely on .env when deploying with Git/GitHub.

Required variables:
DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN=<new bot token>
DISCORD_GUILD_ID=<server ID>
DISCORD_CUSTOMER_ROLE_ID=<customer role ID>

Discord requirements:
1. Add the bot to the same server.
2. Give the bot Manage Roles permission.
3. In Server Settings > Roles, drag the BOT ROLE ABOVE the Customer role.
4. The customer must already be a member of the Discord server.
5. Restart/redeploy Render after changing Environment values.

How to test:
- Open https://YOUR-SITE/api/health
- discordRole.ok should be true.
- In Admin > Orders, delivered orders show Customer role added / Role failed.
- If a delivered order failed, click Retry Customer Role.

Common failures:
401 = bot token invalid/reset.
403 = bot lacks Manage Roles or its role is below Customer.
404 = wrong Guild/Role ID or customer is not in the server.
